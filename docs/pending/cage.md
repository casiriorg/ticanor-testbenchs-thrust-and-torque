# Propeller guard

Not designed yet. Requirements gathered so far:

- Surrounds the propeller plane for 16" now and 20" later; footprint ideally within 60 × 60 cm.
- Stops blade fragments radially (the direction they leave), open enough axially not to disturb the airflow:
  keep mesh at least a quarter of a diameter away from the disc where possible, and note the cage in the test set-up
  because it changes thrust slightly.
- Built from 2020 profile and steel mesh or polycarbonate; printed parts only as brackets.
- Does not touch the measured (moving) assembly.

Next step: collect reference designs and size the frame.
